Who Is Aashir?

Muhammad Aashir Abbas — known professionally as Aashir Rajpoot — is a digital creator who learns by shipping, turning ideas about technology, security and design into interfaces people can actually open and use.

He's currently studying Cyber Security and has completed a diploma in Information Technology, building small, functional web projects along the way — a gallery, a weather app, a dictionary, an expense tracker, a reading tool — each one a way to pick up a new piece of the stack by finishing something real.
